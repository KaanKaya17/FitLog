### Üçüncü taraflar
- **Supabase**: hesap ve kayıtlarınızın saklandığı veritabanı ve kimlik doğrulama hizmeti.
- **Google Gemini**: yemek fotoğrafından besin tahmini, yapay zekâ koçu ve sohbet özelliklerini kullandığınızda gönderdiğiniz fotoğraf, metin ve gerekli profil özeti (ad, cinsiyet, boy, kilo, hedef) işlenmek üzere Google'a iletilir. Bu özellikleri kullanmazsanız hiçbir veri gönderilmez.
- **Google Firebase**: bildirimler; onayınız varsa analitik ve hata raporları.
- **Open Food Facts**: barkod taradığınızda yalnızca barkod numarası gönderilir.

Bu hizmet sağlayıcıların sunucuları yurt dışında bulunabilir. Verileriniz satılmaz ve reklam amacıyla kullanılmaz.

## English

FitLog ("the app") helps you track meals, water and weight. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. The data controller is the app's developer: [İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI].

### Data we collect
| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Account: email, first and last name, username | Creating your account and signing in |
| Profile: gender, birth year, height, weight, target weight, activity level, goals | Calculating calorie and macro targets |
| Logs: meals, water intake, weight history, favourite meals | Core app functionality |
| AI usage counter | Daily limit to prevent abuse |
| Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Reminders and announcements |
| Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics) | **Only with your consent**, to improve the app |

Your password is never stored in the app; Supabase Auth stores it hashed.

### Third parties
- **Supabase**: database and authentication service storing your account and logs.
- **Google Gemini**: when you use photo nutrition estimates, the AI coach or chat, the photo, text and a short profile summary (name, gender, height, weight, goal) are sent to Google for processing. Nothing is sent if you don't use these features.
- **Google Firebase**: notifications; analytics and crash reports only with consent.
- **Open Food Facts**: only the barcode number is sent when you scan a product.

These providers may process data outside your country. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

### Your rights
- **Access / portability:** Settings → Export my data.
- **Deletion:** Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all logs.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn analytics off any time in Settings → Privacy.
- For any other request (GDPR / KVKK): [İLETİŞİM E-POSTASI].

### Health notice
FitLog is not a medical device; suggestions are for information only.

### Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
